Transfers

Open the transfer from your activity list to see a timeline with four stages: paid, converted, sent to the payout partner and delivered. If a stage has not moved for longer than its stated window, support can chase the payout partner on your behalf.

Any transfer that has not yet been deposited or collected can be cancelled, and the money returns to the method you paid with. Card refunds typically take three to five business days to appear because the refund has to travel back through the card network.

Cancel the transfer if it has not been sent on yet, correct the details and send again. If it has already gone to the payout partner, a recall request can be raised — successful recovery depends on whether the receiving bank has credited the account.

The usual causes are a payment that has not cleared yet, a verification check on an unusual transfer, a bank holiday in the destination country, or a recipient account that cannot accept the credit. The timeline will say which stage is holding things up.

The payout method is fixed once a transfer is submitted. Cancel and resend with the method you want, as long as the funds have not been collected.

Account and verification

A current government photo ID — passport, driving licence or national ID card — plus a selfie to match against it. Some transfers also need proof of address dated within the last three months.

Most rejections come down to a blurred photo, a cropped edge, an expired document, or a name that does not match the account exactly. Resubmitting a clear, uncropped photo in good light resolves the majority of cases.

Complete identity verification to move to the higher tier. Above that, you supply evidence of where the funds come from — payslips, a sale deed or an investment statement. See sending limits for the tiers.

Yes, from account settings. Changing an email triggers a confirmation to both the old and new address, and sending is paused for a short window as a precaution against account takeover.

Withdraw any remaining balance, then request closure from settings. Transaction records are kept for the period local regulation requires even after an account closes.

Payments and pricing

Bank transfer, UPI, debit card, credit card and your own Nabha balance. Bank transfer and UPI carry no surcharge; cards do. The rates and fees page lists each one.

Card issuers sometimes block cross-border payment attempts as a fraud precaution, especially on a first transfer. Calling your bank to authorise the merchant usually clears it. Mismatched billing addresses are the other common cause.

A margin is applied to the mid-market rate, and it varies with the delivery speed you choose. The exact rate is shown before you confirm and locked while you complete payment.

Refunds are issued when you cancel before collection, when a deposit bounces back from the receiving bank, or when a cash pickup goes uncollected for 30 days. They return to the original payment method.

Security and fraud

Treat any unexpected request with suspicion, particularly if it is urgent, involves someone you have only met online, or asks you to pay a fee to release a prize, loan or inheritance. Money sent to a stranger and collected is effectively gone. Read our fraud guidance before sending.

Use a password you have not reused anywhere else, turn on two-factor authentication, and never share a one-time code — nobody legitimate will ever ask for one, including support.

Change your password immediately, sign out of all devices from settings, and contact support so any pending transfers can be frozen while the activity is reviewed.
