Hiring process

Four conversations, about three weeks

We tell you the format of every stage in advance, and we do not ask for unpaid work.

  1. Intro call

    Thirty minutes with a recruiter on the role, the team and your expectations.

  2. Craft interview

    A working session on a problem close to the actual job. No algorithm puzzles.

  3. Team conversations

    Two chats with people you would work alongside day to day.

  4. Offer

    A banded offer with the reasoning behind the number, and time to consider it.
