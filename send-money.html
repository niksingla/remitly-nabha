Payout methods

Four ways for money to land

Availability depends on the destination. The directory lists what each country supports.

Straight into their account

The most common choice. You add the account number and routing details once, and we keep them for future transfers.

  • Available in 130+ destinations
  • Express lands in minutes on most corridors, same day on the rest
  • No limit on how many recipients you save
  • Account details are masked after you save them

What you will need

Recipient's full name as it appears on the account, the account number, and one of: IFSC code, SWIFT/BIC, IBAN, sort code or routing number — depending on the country.

To the wallet on their phone

For recipients who do their day-to-day spending from a mobile wallet rather than a bank account.

  • Only the recipient's registered mobile number is needed
  • Funds usually show up within a minute or two
  • Works in markets where wallets are the primary account

Cash over a counter

Useful when your recipient does not hold an account, or when they simply need notes in hand today.

  • Collected at partner agent locations and participating banks
  • Usually ready to collect within minutes of you paying
  • Recipient brings photo ID and the reference number we text them
  • Uncollected transfers are refunded automatically after 30 days

At the counter

  1. They receive a reference number by text.
  2. They show it with a government photo ID.
  3. The agent hands over the full amount — no deduction.

Delivered to the door

Available on a small number of corridors where a courier brings cash to the recipient's address.

  • Scheduled within a delivery window you pick
  • Courier verifies ID before handing over funds
  • Helpful for recipients with limited mobility
  • Carries a higher fee than counter collection

The flow

What sending actually looks like

  1. 1. Tell us the amount

    Enter what you want to send or what they should receive, and we work backwards from whichever you typed.

  2. 2. Add the recipient

    Their name, payout method and details. Saved recipients appear as a one-tap list next time.

  3. 3. Pick a speed

    Express for minutes, Economy for free. The quoted rate is held while you finish paying.

  4. 4. Pay

    Bank transfer, UPI, debit or credit card. Card payments clear fastest; bank transfers cost least.

  5. 5. Track it

    A status timeline shows paid, converted, sent and delivered, with push updates at each stage.

  6. 6. They collect

    Money lands in their account or wallet, or they collect cash with the reference we text them.
