How spending works

The card picks the cheapest balance

You do not have to think about which currency to use. If you have the currency the merchant charges in, we spend that. If not, we convert from your largest balance and tell you exactly what happened.

  1. Tap or pay online

    Use it like any other card, in person or on the web.

  2. We match the currency

    The matching balance is used first, with no conversion at all.

  3. You get a receipt notification

    Amount, currency, rate used and the balance it came from.
