Documents

What you will be asked for

Large cross-border payments attract more scrutiny, and the fastest transfers are the ones where paperwork is ready before the money moves.

  • Photo ID and proof of your current address
  • Evidence of where the funds came from — a sale deed, payslips, a loan sanction letter or an investment statement
  • The reason for the transfer, with a supporting document such as an invoice, fee demand or sale agreement
  • For business transfers, company registration and the beneficial owner details

Exactly which documents apply depends on your country and the destination. A specialist confirms the list before you start.
