How it works

Four taps from here to their account

    Getting started with Remitly Nabha

    Remitly Nabha is a fictional money-transfer brand built as a front-end practice project. Every page, rate and review you see is sample content made to demonstrate how a cross-border payments site is put together. No accounts exist and no money can be sent.

    In this demo, Express transfers are described as arriving within minutes and Economy transfers within three to five business days. A live service's timings would depend on the destination, the payout method and how quickly your payment clears.

    The calculator on this page applies a small sample margin to a hard-coded reference rate, then adds a flat fee on Express transfers below a threshold. It is a stand-in for real pricing logic so the interface has something to display.

    The interface offers bank transfer, debit card, credit card and UPI as options. Choosing one does nothing in this build — selecting a method simply updates the summary copy.

    The Nabha Business pages show how a payouts product for contractors and vendors would be presented, including batch uploads and an API section. Same caveat applies: it is a mock-up.
